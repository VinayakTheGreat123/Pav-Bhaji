Pav Bhaji

Pav bhaji is made from mashed vegetables cooked with a strong blend of spices and served with toasted pav.

Notes

Potato, tomato, peas and cauliflower are common ingredients, although recipes vary between stalls and homes.

  • Butter, chopped onion, coriander and lemon are usually added when the dish is served.
  • Pav bhaji is made from mashed vegetables cooked with a strong blend of spices and served with toasted pav.
